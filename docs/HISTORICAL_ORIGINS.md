# Historical starting points in Appofasistis

This collection begins with selected surviving documents of the formation of the
modern Greek state. It is not an exhaustive register of all Greek legislation or
a claim that one numbered law is the beginning of all Greek law.

The Parliament's [constitutional history](https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/Vouli-ton-Ellinon/To-Politevma/Syntagmatiki-Istoria/)
distinguishes local revolutionary polities in 1821 from the first national Constitution
in 1822. Its [historical collection catalogue](https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/Vouli-ton-Ellinon/I-Bibliothiki/Koinovouleftiki-Syllogiold/Syntagmata/)
provides the source scans. The National Printing Office's
[Gazette API](https://search.et.gr/el/ypiresia-api-fek/) begins its year range at 1833.
